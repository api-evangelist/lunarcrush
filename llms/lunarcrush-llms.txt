# LunarCrush

> LunarCrush is a real-time social intelligence platform for crypto, stocks, and trending topics. It tracks the social media conversation around 4,000+ assets across X, Reddit, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and News, and turns it into proprietary metrics such as Galaxy Score™ and AltRank™.

## For AI agents and programmatic use

If you are an AI agent, assistant, or automated tool that wants to *query* LunarCrush data (topics, categories, creators, time series, metrics), use the AI-friendly interface at https://lunarcrush.ai — it exposes the same data as clean, machine-readable endpoints (JSON/CSV), plus MCP and CLI access, and its own llms.txt describing every route: https://lunarcrush.ai/llms.txt

The pages below on lunarcrush.com are the human-facing marketing and reference site — use them to understand and summarize what LunarCrush is and offers.

## About

LunarCrush aggregates public posts from X (Twitter), Reddit, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and News into topics and categories, then scores them in real time. Every post is run through topic classification, spam/bot filtering, sentiment scoring, and creator weighting before it reaches a metric, so the numbers reflect cleaned, engagement-weighted activity rather than raw mention counts. Proprietary metrics include Galaxy Score™ (a 0–100 composite of social and market health) and AltRank™ (relative momentum across the market).

## Metrics glossary

Definitions, calculation, and interpretation for every LunarCrush metric:

- [Galaxy Score™](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/galaxy-score): A 0–100 composite combining social activity, sentiment, and market momentum into one real-time health score. Above 50 is above-average.
- [AltRank™](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/altrank): Ranks an asset's combined social and price performance relative to the rest of the market; lower is stronger.
- [Social Volume](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/social-volume): The number of public posts mentioning an asset over a period.
- [Social Dominance](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/social-dominance): An asset's share of the total conversation within its category.
- [Sentiment](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/sentiment): The engagement-weighted emotional tone (bullish vs bearish) of the conversation.
- [Social Mentions](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/social-mentions): The raw count of posts mentioning an asset.
- [Social Engagement](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/social-engagement): Total interactions (likes, replies, reposts, views) on posts about an asset.
- [Social Contributors](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/social-contributors): The number of distinct accounts posting about an asset.
- [Market Dominance](https://lunarcrush.com/metrics/market-dominance): An asset's share of total market capitalization within its category.
